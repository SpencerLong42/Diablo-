## 0. Master checklist (do these in order)

### Phase 1: tonight (finish the story, get set up)
- [ ] Finish the seasonal quests **The Burning Shepherd**, **The Brother's Keeper** and **Legacy of Hope**. At the end, choose to keep the **Splinter of Destruction** (§1).
- [ ] Open the **Season Rank** screen and claim everything. Clear any Capstones you haven't done: Rank I is **Halls of the Blind** (Expert+) and Rank II is **Den of Evil** (Penitent+) (§3).
- [ ] Respec into the **Starter** setup: skills, Legion Shard, gear, runewords (§6).
- [ ] Go to **Temis** (Skovos). Set up a **War Plan** at Tyrael's table and check the **Horadric Cube** (§4).
- [ ] Equip a **Seal** and fill it with charms (§4.3, §6).
- [ ] Put **Soul Splinters** in your amulet and rings (§6).
- [ ] Hire **Varyana** and set **Raheir** as your reinforcement (§4.5, §6).
- [ ] Spend Paragon points: Start board, then Dynamism, Fathomless, Demonic Spicules and Greater Hex. Socket **Superiority** first (§10).
- [ ] Spend **Smoldering Ashes** on **Urn of Reclamation** now and **Urn of Glyphs** next (§3.2).

### Phase 2: unlock Torment (day 1–2)
- [ ] Clear **Pit tier 10** at the Artificer's Obelisk in **Cerrigar**. That unlocks **Torment I**; switch to it.
- [ ] Rank III Capstone: **Scouring of Caldeum** (Torment I+).
- [ ] Clear **Pit 15** to unlock Torment II. Get one glyph to level 10 as well, for a Rank IV objective. Then do the Rank IV Capstone, **Horadric Library** (Torment II+).
- [ ] Clear **Pit 25** to unlock Torment IV. Get three glyphs to 20 as well, for a Rank V objective. Then do the Rank V Capstone, **Worldstone Keep (Nightmare)**. This Baal dungeon is also where **Leoric's Crown** drops.

### Phase 3: farm the core uniques (Initiate Lair Bosses, 1 Lair Key each)
- [ ] **Lord Zir** drops **Elegy** (the build's key item).
- [ ] **Urivar** drops **Infernal Homunculus**.
- [ ] **Beast in the Ice** drops **The Eightfold Idol**.
- [ ] Any Lair Boss can drop **Temerity** and **Wendigo Brand** from the general pool.
- [ ] Get **Leoric's Crown** from the Season 15 Capstone Waking Nightmares.
- [ ] Once you have **Elegy and Infernal Homunculus**, switch to the **Midgame** setup (§7).

### Phase 4: Greater bosses, Mythics, polish (Torment VI+)
- [ ] **Butcher** drops **Moloch's Beating Flame**. **Harbinger of Hatred** drops Temerity. **Bartuc** drops **Hand of Apotheosis**; you reach him with 666 Burning Aether in Infernal Hordes.
- [ ] Craft **Enigma** (Jah + Ith + Ber) if you want the Teleport playstyle (§9, §12).
- [ ] **Masterwork** everything to 25, weapons first, and steer the capstones. Use Greater Affix tempers.
- [ ] Mythic priority: **Moloch's, then Temerity, then Leoric's, then Elegy, then Infernal Homunculus**. Only one Mythic made with the Cube's Upgrade to Mythic recipe can be equipped (§12.5).
- [ ] Level all 5 glyphs to 50, then 100, then 150 (§10).

### Phase 5: season finish line
- [ ] Clear Rank VI (Durance of Hate, Nightmare, T6), VII (Labyrinth, Nightmare, T8), VIII (Echo of Mephisto on T10) and IX (10 of 24 challenges).
- [ ] Get the Splinter of Destruction to **rank 24** (20,000 reputation).
- [ ] Optional: swap to the Splinter of Hatred for Pit and glyph grinding. Try Uber Tristram for the Hellfire Torch (§15).

Deckard Cain gives the quests at the **Imperial Library (Caldeum, Kehjistan)**. You already have the Splinter of Destruction, so quest 2 is done.

| # | Quest | Where it starts | What you fight | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Refuge from the Memories ✅ | Kuhuthi's Tavern, **Tarsarak** | Demons in the tavern, then you meet Cain | – |

The game warns you: *"You will be bound to your Prime Evil for some time."* Cain lets you keep only one splinter.

### Which splinter to choose: keep Destruction

If you already chose it, skip ahead. Here's why it's the right pick for you now:

- **It speeds up exactly what you need to farm.** Destruction boosts **the Undercity and Lair Bosses**. Your key uniques drop from Lair Bosses: Elegy (Lord Zir), Infernal Homunculus (Urivar), The Eightfold Idol (Beast in the Ice), Moloch's (Butcher) and Temerity (Harbinger). Its track adds extra Boss Trophies, extra runes from Lair Bosses, more Nemesis Lair portals, and **+3 extra Uniques in Hoard chests** (§2).
- **Its Iconic Power helps this build.** At rank 6 every proc gives **+50 Wrath**. At rank 16 procs apply **Vulnerable**, and this build scales heavily off Vulnerable.
- **It's the standard plan.** Guides agree: use Destruction while gearing, and consider **Hatred** (Pit and glyph bonuses) only once you're geared and just pushing the Pit.

| Splinter | Prime Evil | Element | Activities it boosts | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Destruction** (yours) | Baal | Cold | **Undercity, Lair Bosses**, Whispers | Farming uniques and leveling |
| Terror | Diablo | Fire | Helltide, Infernal Hordes, Whispers | Helltide/Hordes players |
| Hatred | Mephisto | Physical | Pit, Nightmare Dungeons, Whispers | Glyphs, Pit pushing, Resplendent Sparks |

**Swapping later:**
- Splinters are consumable items. Right-click a different one to replace your current splinter. **The item is used up**, so switching back means farming another.
- Extra splinters are rare. The best source is the Capstone Waking Nightmares. The Baal one, "Worldstone Keep (Hell)" in Fractured Peaks, clears in about 2–4 minutes.
- Reputation is tracked per splinter. One source says the other tracks keep their ranks, but that isn't fully confirmed.

**After you pick:**
- You'll get **Prime Evil Ambushes**. After local events or Whispers, you get a 10-second warning, then an Echo of Baal, Diablo or Mephisto spawns. They drop loot.
- Carrying a splinter also makes you "more vulnerable to its Prime Evil", so expect Baal's echoes to hit harder.

## 3. Season Rank ladder (Season Journey)

There are **9 ranks and 125+ objectives**. To advance a rank you clear its **Capstone**. Most Capstones are a recreated classic Waking Nightmare. Across all ranks you earn:
- **14 Skill Points and 42 Paragon Points.** Your build assumes you have them: 83 skill points in total.
- **10 Resplendent Sparks** and **5 Mythic Unique Caches**.
- Lair Key Rings, Forgotten Souls, Obducite and Neathiron.

Check your Season Rank screen. You're probably already partway through.

| Rank | Capstone | Pit / glyph gates in its objectives | Notable rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Halls of the Blind** (Expert+) | – | 1 Skill Point, Archetype Cache, Smoldering Ashes |
| II | **Den of Evil** (Penitent+) | – | 2 Skill Points, **Lair Key**, Smoldering Ashes. War Plans reputation starts here. |
| III | **Scouring of Caldeum** (Torment I+) | Pit 10, which unlocks T1 | 3 Skill Points, 4 Paragon, **Lair Key Ring**, Ashes |
| IV | **Horadric Library** (Torment II+) | Pit 15 (T2) and **one glyph at 10** | 3 Skill Points, 4 Paragon, **Greater Lair Key Ring**, "Banner of the Three" mount trophy |
| V | **Worldstone Keep – Nightmare** (Torment IV+) | Pit 25 (T4), **three glyphs at 20**, masterwork 10 items to quality 10, temper 16 Ancestral Legendaries | 4 Paragon, **Superior Lair Key Ring**, Mystery Greater Archetype Cache |
| VI | **Durance of Hate – Nightmare** (Torment VI+) | Clear six standard Waking Nightmares on **T5** | **1 Resplendent Spark**, 4 Paragon, **Dark Wanderbear** pet, 800 Forgotten Soul |
| VII | **The Labyrinth – Nightmare** (Torment VIII+) | Pit 60 (T8), **three glyphs at 45**, Tormented Echo of Harbinger, Duriel and Andariel hoards on T7 | 4 Paragon, **Crux of the False Prophet** (summons Mephisto), **Mythic Unique Journey Cache** |
| VIII | **Echo of Mephisto on Torment X** (needs Lord of Hatred) | Pit 80 (T10), **three glyphs at 50**, **Paragon 250**, kill all three Echoes on T10 | 1 Resplendent Spark, Mythic Unique Journey Cache, title "Deckard's Confidant" |
| IX | **Complete 10 of 24 challenges** (mostly T11–T12) | – | **3 Resplendent Sparks**, Mythic Unique Archetype Cache, 4 Neathiron, 25 Volatile Primordial Dust |

**Fastest order:**
1. Rush Ranks I–III first, for the skill points and the Rank III Lair Key Ring.
2. Aim for **Rank V** next (Torment IV), then level your glyphs.
3. Push to **Rank VIII**, which is the practical finish line.
4. Run the Capstone Waking Nightmares, since they complete objectives for several ranks at once. They're also your main source of splinter reputation and Leoric's Crown.

### 3.2 Season Blessings (Smoldering Ashes)
You earn Smoldering Ashes from objectives and Capstones, and spend them on season-long buffs.
1. **Urn of Reclamation:** better chance of rare materials from salvage. It's the best pick while you're still gearing.
2. **Urn of Glyphs:** a chance for an extra glyph upgrade in the Pit. It's the best long-term pick, since glyph levels gate Ranks IV–VIII.
3. **Urn of Masterworking:** a chance for extra **Obducite**.
4. Urn of Curiosities (a second item from Purveyor gambles) and Urn of Ancestral Whispers (Ancestral caches from the Tree of Whispers) when you can afford them.

## 4. Systems to set up right after the campaign

### 4.1 Temis (Skovos), the Lord of Hatred hub
- **War Plans:** talk to **Tyrael** and use the table next to him.
- **Horadric Cube:** unlocked in the campaign quest "Doorways to the Distant Past". It lives in Temis.
- **Echoing Hatred:** an endless horde mode, entered with a **Trace of Echoes** at the **Sightless Eye**. It's a final exam for a finished build, not a gearing activity.

### 4.2 War Plans: set one up and never play outside it
- **What it is:** a playlist of up to **5 activities** chosen from Tree of Whispers, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides, Undercity, Lair Bosses, Infernal Hordes and the Pit. "New Plans" rerolls the options up to 3 times.
- **Key rule:** **Activity XP only counts for activities in your active War Plan.** Each activity has its own tree with 7 levels and 1 point per level. The Whispers tree levels from open-world Whispers.
- **Suggested plan for you:** **Undercity, Lair Bosses, the Pit, Helltide, Nightmare Dungeons.** Swap in Infernal Hordes while you're farming Bartuc.
  - The Lair Boss tree needs **Torment I+**.
  - Greater Lair Boss nodes need **Torment VI+**.

**Node order (Maxroll's recommendations; ★ = gets a bonus from your Destruction splinter):**

| Tree | Take in this order |
|---|---|
| Lair Bosses | **Two by Two★**, Greater Nemesis, **Lair of Runes★** (runes from bosses, including Jah/Nagu from Beast in the Ice), then **Exotic Armory★** |
| Undercity | **Endless Swarm★**, Unfortunate Souls, Gutter Filth, **Jade Epiphany★** (XP), Finely Tuned, Pathfinder, Initiative; **Last Gasp★** also gets a Destruction bonus |
| Pit | Choron's Blessing, Choron's Haste (extra glyph upgrades), Heart of Stone, Damned Thieves |
| Helltide | Hellmouth, Writhe and Rot (big XP plus extra Lair Keys), Wretched Vermin |
| Nightmare Dungeons | Waking Spoils, Gauntlet, Fearless Conviction, Nemesis, Dread Revival, … |
| Whispers | Tree of Plenty, Wisdom of Whispers, Grim Mysteries, Resplendent Favor, **Grim Retribution★**, **Colossal Foe★** |

⚠️ Maxroll's War Plans guide (Aug 2026) warns that the Lair Boss node **"Infernal Deception"** was bugged: the Belial it spawns dropped no loot. Skip it unless you've confirmed it's fixed.

### 4.3 Talismans (Seal + Charms)
- The **Seal** sets how many charm slots you get: Magic 3, Rare 4, **Legendary 5**, Mythic 6. Some Seals roll **"+1 Charm Slot"**. You can't use charms without a Seal.
- **Set charms** give set bonuses. This build uses **Abaddon's Flesh** (Warlock), plus **the Nameless** at endgame.
- **Unique charms** carry a Unique item's power. Since S15 they can also be Mythic, adding +30% to that power.
- Salvage spare charms and Seals at the **Occultist** for **Infused Horadric Resin**.
- **Reroll Set Charm** in the Cube is how you get Greater Affixes on charms.

### 4.4 Horadric Cube: recipes you'll actually use

| Recipe | Use |
|---|---|
| **Craft Unique**: Common item + 1 Enhanced Dust + 10 Raw Dust | Random Unique of that slot. Use an Ancestral white base for an Ancestral Unique. Good for rings: Wendigo Brand and The Eightfold Idol. |
| **Boss Trophies**: 5 identical | A random Unique from that boss's table. Elegy, Homunculus, Idol and Moloch's can all come this way. |
| **3-to-1 Transmutation** | 3 same-type items make a random new one. Three Ancestral inputs give an Ancestral output. |
| **Upgrade to Legendary** | A Rare becomes a Legendary with a random power. An early source of aspects. |
| **Upgrade to Mythic**: Ancestral 900-IP Unique + **5 Resplendent Sparks** | Keeps all stats, Greater Affixes, tempers and masterwork. **Only 1 "Crafted" Mythic can be equipped. Doesn't work on unmodifiable (transfigured) items.** |
| **Runewords** | Put a white base plus the runes in the Cube: Stealth, Ancient's Pledge, Enigma (§12.4) |
| **Rune upgrades**, e.g. 3 Tir → Eth | See §12.4 |
| **Amalgamation** | Soul Splinters of the same type and rarity make the next rarity. 5 Grand gems make a Horadric gem. 5 Magic or Rare runes make a random higher rune. |
| **Affix Value Reroll** | Rerolls the values of normal affixes. Greater Affixes, tempers and enchants are protected. |
| **Transfigure** (Volatile Dust) | Do this last. It usually locks the item. A **Kullean prism** adds a random Utility aspect to an amulet (§8). |

### 4.5 Mercenaries (The Den, Nahantu)
- **Raheir** (shieldbearer tank) joins in the Vessel of Hatred campaign.
- **Varyana** (berserker): pick up the "Brutal Note" in The Den after clearing the **Temple of Rot** stronghold (Dry Steppes). It starts the quest "Slayer's Retribution".
- **Aldkin** (cursed child): quest "A Nameless Mystery" in Nahantu.
- **Subo** (archer): quest "A Feather on the Scale" in Hawezar. This build doesn't use him.
- You get **1 hired mercenary plus 1 reinforcement**. Set the reinforcement to trigger on a skill you cast often.
- The Season Rank I objective asks you to get any mercenary to level 3.

### 4.6 Codex of Power, Occultist and Jeweler
- **Aspects:** salvage Legendaries to add their aspects to your Codex, then **Imprint** them at the Occultist.
  - Non-Ancestral: 15 Baleful Fragment + 250k gold.
  - Ancestral: 25 Forgotten Soul + 1M gold.
- **Enchanting (Occultist):** rerolls one affix per item. The item becomes account-bound, and the gold cost climbs with each reroll.
- **Sockets (Jeweler):** Ancestral items cost 1 Scattered Prism + 150k gold. Helms, chests and pants can have 2 sockets.

### 4.7 The Pit and Torment tiers
- **Where:** the **Artificer's Obelisk, just north of the Cerrigar waypoint** (Scosglen).
- **Entry cost:** **Artificer's Stones**, from Whispers, Nightmare Dungeons, Helltides and Legion events.
- **The rule:** you only unlock the next Torment tier by clearing its Pit tier. Capstones don't unlock Torment.

| Torment | Pit clear needed | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| I | 10 (at level 70) | **Ancestral items and Greater Affixes** start dropping. Lair Bosses open. |
| II | 15 | More Legendary temper manuals and Scrolls of Restoration |
| III | 20 | More set charms |
| IV | 25 | More Neathiron |
| V | 30 | More Legendary Runes |
| VI | 40 | **More Greater Lair Keys** (Butcher, Harbinger) |
| VII | 50 | More Volatile Primordial Dust |
| VIII | 60 | More Unique Charms |
| IX | 70 | More Kullean Tuning Prisms |
| X | 80 | More Mythic Horadric Seals and better Mythic Tribute odds. **A realistic long-term farming tier.** |
| XI | 90 | Mostly more XP and gold |
| XII | 100 | Maximum XP |

- **Mythic drop chance scales with Torment**, from about 0.05% at T1 to about 0.4% at T12. The source says these numbers are its own inference.
- **Unlock as high as you can push, but farm at the highest tier that is still fast.**

## 5. How Blazing Scream works

- **The skulls.** You cast **Blazing Scream** (Hellfire Core skill) and demon skulls travel out, **bouncing off walls and terrain** and hitting enemies again. **Aim into corridors. Against bosses, aim at a wall behind them.** It's a big DPS gain.
- **Elegy** (sword) makes Blazing Scream an **Archfiend** skill and gives it the **Skull Splitter** variant for free. In S15 it also makes the small skulls deal the **same damage as the main skull**.
- **Infernal Homunculus** (focus) makes every Archfiend cast trigger all your **Lesser Demon upgrades** for free, lasting 200% longer. That includes **Wrath generation and Vulnerable**, the two most important.
- **The Eightfold Idol** (ring) makes Blazing Scream leave **Sigil of Subversion** trails. That counts as casting a Sigil, which powers the Nameless charm set and **Sigil of Lava**.
- **Metamorphosis: Destruction Demon** is the high-risk multiplier.
  - It gives permanent Demonform, plus **Volatility**, which **Moloch's Beating Flame** needs.
  - Each Hellfire cast **raises your Hellfire damage but burns you for 8% Max Life**.
  - You survive the burn with **Temerity** (overhealing becomes Barrier), **Sadistic Aspect** (heals on demon deaths) and **Undying Aspect** (heals on cast).
- **Splinter of the Black Soulstone.** Every demon death, and Blazing Scream's lesser skulls count as demons, stacks souls. At the Abyssal tier that's up to +500% damage, but you also take more damage. **Bombardment** stacks it very fast.
- **Strengths:** very mobile, huge AoE, an easy transition from leveling.
- **Weaknesses:** constant skill spam, you have to aim, and you juggle two resources (Wrath and Dominance).

- Run the **fastest Pit tier that is 10+ levels above your glyph**, and never die.

## 12. Crafting: tempering, masterworking, sockets, runewords, Mythics

### 12.1 Order to work on an item ("S.T.E.A.M.")
1. Socket
2. Temper
3. Enchant
4. Aspect
5. Masterwork
6. Transfigure (last, and optional)

Build the affixes on a Magic or Rare base first. Imprinting an aspect makes the item Legendary.

### 12.2 Tempering

| Temper this build uses | Manual |
|---|---|
| Maximum Life | **Worldly Endurance** |
| Lucky Hit: Restore Primary Resource | **Worldly Stability** (resource tempers go on jewelry only) |
| Resource Cost Reduction | **Worldly Stability** |
| Critical Strike Chance and Attack Speed | **Worldly Destruction** |
| Demonology Damage | **Demonologist** (Warlock manual) |
| Movement Speed | **Natural Motion** |

- **One temper per item.** You get 3 reroll charges, plus 1 per Greater Affix, up to 7. The temper itself can roll as a Greater Affix (+50%).

- Rare and Legendary manuals drop in Torment.

### 12.3 Masterworking (Blacksmith)
- **How it works:** Quality goes from 0 to 25, adding **+1% to all affixes per point**. At 25 you get a **Capstone**: +50% to one random affix, which stacks with a Greater Affix.
- **Rerolling the Capstone:**
  - 850 IP: 100 Obducite + 10M gold.
  - 900 IP Ancestral: 200 Obducite + 1 **Neathiron** + 10M gold.
  - Two-handed weapons cost double.
- **Priorities:**
  - **Weapons first.**
  - Don't masterwork until the item is Ancestral.
  - Steer capstones onto the "MW cap" affixes in §8.1.
- **Obducite sources, best first:**
  1. Nightmare Dungeon sigils with **Treasure Breach**.
  2. Strongroom sigils.
  3. Undercity with **Tribute of Refinement**.
  4. Infernal Hordes.
  5. Masterworking Caches bought by bartering with mercenaries.

## 13. Survival checklist
- **Barrier engine:** Temerity + Sadistic Aspect + Undying Aspect. All the overhealing becomes Barrier. **You need all three to survive Destruction Demon's self-burn.**
- **Fire Resistance above 10,000.** Metamorphosis burns you with Fire, and Aspect of Scorching Heat scales from Fire Resistance.
- **Damage reduction sources:**
  - Dark Prison: Chain Aura (45% from outside).
  - Aspect of Diabolical Armor.
  - The Abaddon's Flesh seal bonus in Demonform.
  - **Splinter of Pain** (25% damage reduction but −30% movement speed; **Splinter of the Mother's Unhindered** cancels the slow).
  - **Lac** (taunt with damage reduction) and **Que** (Barrier).
  - **Raheir** and **Aldkin**.
- **Black Soulstone raises the damage you take** as its stacks grow. **Don't use it on Hardcore.**
- **Max Life on nearly every item.** Metamorphosis also scales your damage off bonus Maximum Life.
- **If you keep dying:** go back one Torment tier, check your resistances, and move Paragon points toward defense.

## 14. Your repeating endgame loop
1. Claim finished **Season Rank**, **Whisper** and **War Plan** rewards.
2. Run your **War Plan**: Undercity, Lair Bosses, Pit, Helltide and Nightmare Dungeons. This levels the activity trees and your Destruction reputation.
3. **Use keys only on bosses whose uniques you still need.** Greater keys and Husks go on Greater bosses and Belial.
4. **Pit** for glyphs, and for the next Torment unlock when you're ready.
5. **Capstone Waking Nightmares** for splinter reputation, Season Rank and Leoric's Crown.
6. Craft (temper, masterwork, Cube), then test the next tier. Drop back if your clear time doubles.
7. Every **5 matching Boss Trophies**, craft a Unique in the Cube.

  - Surprise one-room pop-ups, such as Queen Araneae or Rakanishu.
  - Rank IV has an objective to collect 5 different Soul Splinter types and use Chronicles on Waking Nightmares.
- **Soul Splinters in general:**
  - Up to 3 go in jewelry, plus 2 in Leoric's Crown.
  - Upgrade them by combining same-type, same-rarity splinters (Cube Amalgamation), from Lesser up to **Abyssal**.
  - **Lies** gives +100% gold and materials and +25% XP, but you're always Weakened and Vulnerable.
  - **Damnation** raises Monster Power and XP after elite kills. It's the Endgame XP splinter.
- **Moophisto** (World Boss, "The Divine Bovine") drops the "Bell of the Bovine" amulet. There's also a Cow Level. Both are just for fun.

## 16. Mistakes to avoid
- ❌ Equipping Infernal Homunculus before you have Elegy.
- ❌ Using a non-Mythic, or under-90%, Hand of Apotheosis. Aspect of Ignition is better.
- ❌ Transfiguring an item you still plan to upgrade to Mythic in the Cube.
- ❌ Making two Cube "Crafted" Mythics. Only one can be equipped.
- ❌ Farming the highest Torment you've unlocked instead of the fastest one that's safe.
- ❌ Running activities outside your War Plan. You lose activity XP.
- ❌ Spending Lair Keys on bosses that don't drop what you need.
- ❌ Firing Blazing Scream into open space against bosses. Use a wall.
- ❌ Running Destruction Demon without Temerity + Sadistic (+ Undying). The self-burn will kill you.
- ❌ Swapping splinters casually. It uses up the splinter item.
